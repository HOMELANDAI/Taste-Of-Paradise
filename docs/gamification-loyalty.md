# Gamification and Loyalty

Users earn points for Heat Votes, ingredient votes, recipe submissions, taste testing, reviews, shares, live event attendance, sponsor campaign participation, collectible unlocks, referrals, and venue QR scans.

## Loyalty Tiers

Newcomer, Taster, Heat Sender, Flavor Builder, VIP, Legacy T.O.P Member.

## Badges

First Heat Vote, Seared Cake Supporter, T.O.P Cup Collector, Burnt Ends Tester, Linzer Signature Taster, Paradise Menu Lab Builder, VIP First Taste, Sponsor Drop Hunter, Recipe Vault Explorer, HAI Cookbook Collector, NCRD Explorer.

## Leaderboards

Top Heat Voters, Top Flavor Builders, Top Taste Testers, Most Collected Cards, Most Shared Recipes, Top VIP Members.
