# Concept Overview

**Taste of Paradise / T.O.P by HAI** is a HomelandAI rooftop food innovation platform. It combines live cooking, tropical hospitality, social tasting, sponsor-ready menu drops, gamified recipe development, and collectible cookbook cards.

## Format

- Weekly rooftop test kitchen episodes
- Family-led or chef-led preparation
- Community recipe voting
- Small in-person taste tester groups
- Reaction filming and social clips
- Sponsor-backed ingredient and sauce drops
- Recipe archive and database

## Differentiator

Traditional food content is passive. T.O.P creates a loop: propose a dish, vote on ingredients, test it live, film reactions, move winners into the Signature Menu, and unlock collectible HAI Cookbook cards.

## Audience

Local Dominican Republic food lovers, rooftop nightlife guests, livestream viewers, food creators, sponsor brands, VIP members, recipe collectors, and North Coast RD Live users.
