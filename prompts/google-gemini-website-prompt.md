# Google Gemini Studio Website Prompt

Build a premium responsive website for **Taste of Paradise / T.O.P by HAI**, a HomelandAI culinary innovation concept. The site must function as a luxury rooftop tropical test kitchen website, recipe showcase, signature menu archive, live-stream companion, sponsor-ready promotional hub, taste tester signup platform, and NCRD Gateway.

## Visual Style

Use a vivid Figma-inspired layout: dark navy, near-black, metallic gold, amber-copper cooking glow, thin cyan accent lines, tropical palm and hibiscus motifs, rooftop string-light bokeh, parchment recipe cards, rounded premium panels, glossy food photography references, and neon badges for live and limited-drop elements.

## Pages

Home, Signature Menu, Paradise Menu Lab, Recipe Vault, Live Sear Stage, Taste Tester Waitlist, Limited Drops, Sponsors & Collaborations, HAI Cookbook Collectibles, NCRD Gateway, About, Contact, Admin Dashboard.

## Global Header

Sticky header with T.O.P by HAI logo, navigation links, Login/Profile, and a scrolling announcement ticker.

Ticker examples: LIVE THIS WEEK: Seared Cake Classic enters the HAI Cookbook pipeline. NEW DROP: Sugarcane Kola Cantaloupe Luxe. VIP FIRST TASTE: Join the Taste Tester Waitlist. NORTH COAST RD LIVE: Explore local tastings and rooftop experiences.

## Key Functions

Create filterable signature menus, searchable recipe vault, taste tester forms, sponsor pages, social icons, live stream placeholders, Heat Votes, Ingredient Voting Matrix, Flavor Profile Ring, collectible recipe cards, admin settings, and NCRD Gateway.

## Backend Models

Establishments, Users, Recipes, Collections, MenuItems, LiveEpisodes, TechniqueStages, IngredientVotes, HeatVotes, FlavorProfiles, TasteTesterQueue, Sponsors, Campaigns, CollectibleCards, LoyaltyEvents, Badges, UserRewards, MediaAssets, Comments, Notifications, DropCards, SocialLinks, NCRDGatewayContent, AnalyticsEvents.

## Troubleshooting States

Recipe not found, stream unavailable, voting closed, duplicate vote blocked, admin permission denied, image failed to load, collectible not unlocked, taste tester form incomplete, NCRD gateway unavailable.
